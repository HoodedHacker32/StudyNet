What’s new

November 2026

Just added

  • Build your own practice exam: choose how many questions, how long each one is, which kinds of question, your own time limit, and whether a calculator is allowed.
  • A new, realistic calculator, with a screen that shows numbers the way a real one does. On a laptop it sits beside the page so it never covers a question.
  • Maths is here: practise every algebra skill from class with a new question each time, hints and worked examples, and move up from Learning to Mastered. Type answers any correct way: (x + 2)(x + 3) and (x+3)(x+2) both count.
  • Practice exams for every subject, laid out like your class tests. Pick your topics and length, sit the paper with or without a timer, then get it back marked like a real test, with your Junior Cycle descriptor.
  • Maths papers give method marks for correct working, even if your final answer is wrong.
  • Written answers and maths working can be marked by AI against the marking scheme. Otherwise, you tick off the scheme yourself.
  • Science: a periodic table with Bohr diagrams for the first 20 elements, and much more on the structure of the atom.
  • A calculator on every page: it's in the menu.
  • A new About page explains exactly how StudyNet was made and how AI is used.
  • Each subject has its own logo and colour, and a new, easier-to-read font.

2nd Year

  • 2nd Year content for Gaeilge, Français, History, Geography and Science, built around this term’s November assessments.
  • In 2nd Year mode you can still switch back to your 1st Year topics on each subject.
  • New Notes pages for each topic, plus timelines in History.

Easier to use

  • Jump straight to any of your subjects from the side menu (or the menu button on your phone).
  • The home page takes you back to exactly where you left off.
  • Open Settings from any page.
  • Practice has a Check answers button and shows the right answer if you get one wrong.
